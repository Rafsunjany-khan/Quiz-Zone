Guidelines for Quiz Zone

Welcome to Quiz Zone! To get the best experience while using our platform, please follow these guidelines:

  1. Register and login to access all quizzes.
  2. Always complete the quiz by answering all questions before submitting.
  3. You can only rate a quiz after completing it.
  4. Do not refresh the page during a quiz attempt to avoid losing your answers.
  5. Check your internet connection to ensure smooth quiz submission.
  6. Respect other users and do not share quiz answers publicly.
  7. Contact support for any technical issues.
